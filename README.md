# Tide-Weaver

Surf endless twilight waves as a paper-winged light spirit. Hold to dive down the slopes, let go to fly off the crests. Every dive, ring and apex plays a note, so every run composes its own song.

A coralctrl game. Free, in the browser, no sign-up. Play: https://tide-weaver.vishwanathanirv.workers.dev/

## Play

- **In the air, hold** to backflip; let go and the board settles to your flight path. Land flat: clean flips bank big points. Land upside down and you shatter, back on the wave in 0.1 s.
- **Dive into the abyss:** hold down a steep slope at speed to pierce the surface. Under water, collect sunken gems, ride warm jets, and let go to breach skyward.
- **Trick chains:** backflips, breaches, rings, gems and split-ring threads chain together and bank on a clean landing.

- **Press to start.** The spirit glides forward on its own; your only control is when to press.
- **Hold** (Space, click or touch) to dive down a slope and gain speed. Let go at the bottom to fly off the next crest.
- **Perfect dive:** land along a downslope. It builds your streak and speeds you up.
- **Zen earns nothing:** no gems and no goals, since nothing there can hurt you.
- **Surge:** a burst of speed and a ring magnet for a little over a second, for 12% of your light. It recharges in 4.5 s, works on the ground and in the air, and is free in Zen.
- **Doing nothing costs light:** after about 3 seconds with no press the light drains up to four times faster, distance earns little, and your tier stops climbing. A long jump still counts as play; only sitting still does not.
- **Your light** fades as you ride. Perfect dives and rings refill it. When it goes out, the run ends and tells you why.
- **Flow tiers:** ride without a rough landing to climb from Calm to Flowing, Radiant and Transcendent. Each tier raises the multiplier and brightens your trail.

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Space, click, touch | Dive |
| Shift, X, right-click, second finger, or the Surge button on a touch screen | Surge |
| C | Save the last 10 seconds as a clip |
| Space on the summary | Ride again |
| P or Esc | Pause |
| M | Sound |
| R | Hide the interface (for clean recordings) |

## The music grows with you

- Lo-fi keys and vinyl crackle from the first press.
- **×1.5:** swung hi-hats and a soft kick join in.
- **×3.0, LOCKED IN:** a bass drop, cinema bars, and the waves pulse with the bassline.
- Surfing the water makes a soft skim that rises with your speed.
- Callouts react to how you move: **clean** for a dead-centre ring (with a 30 ms freeze) or a razor-sharp landing, **inches** for a near miss, **skill issue** for a belly flop.

## Biomes

- **0 to 1 km, Twilight Shallows:** rolling swells and rings.
- **1 to 2 km, Mirage Straits:** trick rings that split at the last second and crests that collapse under you.
- **2 km on, Obsidian Storm:** crystal spikes and tide gates that move on the beat.

Zen mode keeps every biome but nothing can hurt you.

## Modes

- **Journey:** keep your light burning as long as you can. A pillar of light marks your best distance.
- **Today's wave:** the same wave for everyone until midnight UTC, with a shareable result.
- **Zen:** no light, no ending. Just the sea and the music.

## What changes as you play

- **Five skies, one per minute:** watercolour twilight, a moonlit sea, aurora, paper-cut dawn and ink-wash rain, crossfading as real changes of paint.
- **A camera with moods:** it sits close, leads where you are going, rolls with the slope, pushes in on dives, leans on impacts and drifts slowly while LOCKED IN.
- **A world with depth:** a lighthouse, sea stack, arch, ruins or wreck every kilometre on a far layer, and at every fifth kilometre a colossal gate with the ribs of something enormous. Dark reeds drift past in front. Mist gathers in the troughs, pollen, embers and spores drift by, and ink rain rings the water.
- **Traces:** foam stays on the crests you carved, and rings you fly through break into arcs that float on the sea.
- **The water answers:** a shallow, perfect entry skims with a silky hiss; slamming in flat brings a sub thud, a tall column of water and a shake. Up high the score thins to air and chimes; deep down it warms and a whale-like swell sings.
- **The rider is the meter:** the scarf shortens and dims as your light fades, and a shimmer runs down the board when a Surge is ready.
- **Goals:** three goals are always active (breach, dead-centre rings, double backflips, Surges and more). Finish all three to rank up, earn gems and get a harder set.
- **Upgrades:** three gem upgrades, Lv 1 to 5, the first for 15 to 25 gems. Light Vessel (+8% light duration per level), Tide Magnet (wider ring and gem pull, even outside Surge), Surge Core (recharge from 4.5 s toward 2.8 s, cheaper in light).
- **Hooks:** a Daily chest on the title (10 to 25 gems, once a day), Start Boost (+30 launch speed per level), rider skins (Ronin, Kitsune, Storm Valkyrie), and Second Wind: 25 gems to continue a faded run once. Runs that use it are not shareable as ghosts.
- **Juice:** a shockwave ring on every ring, foam bursts on clean landings, CLOSE! +100 with a brief slow-motion on near misses, a sub thump and bell on dead-centre rings, swipe up or double-tap to Surge on touch.
- **How to play:** a small ? button (or H) opens a one-screen guide with a looping picture.
- **Stats and portals:** anonymous play stats (first run, second run, where runs end) are off unless the build has `VITE_POSTHOG_KEY`; players can switch them off in Settings. On Poki or CrazyGames the portal SDK adds a free rewarded Second Wind, and gameplay start and stop are signalled; elsewhere those calls do nothing.
- **Opening:** the first 450 m is scripted: a valley to dive for speed, a climb to a lip, a low Sky-Rail you launch straight into, four rings and a whirlpool, with no traps. A plain dive-the-downhills rider reaches the rail on about two waves in three.
- **Wonders:** meteor splash-ramps with gold shards, abyssal caverns with a crystal gem trail, a wandering rival spirit to pass for a gem, and a coyote window (let go up to 0.12 s late over a crest and you still launch).
- **Music:** a four-chord D Lydian bed (Dmaj9, E6/D, C#m7, F#m9) with five stems that crossfade as you ride: felt piano always, harp with speed, sub-bass and brushes from x1.5, glass bells and air with altitude, a 250 Hz low-pass and cello underwater. Chimes duck it for 80 ms.
- **Feel:** a rising pitch chain on rings and clean landings (resets on a slam), short haptic clicks on touch devices (Settings), larger score and goals on phones.
- **Workshop:** spend abyss gems on boards that change handling (Anchor dives harder, Feather floats and flips fast) and scarves that lengthen the scarf and the light trail.
- **Ghost races:** the Challenge link carries your whole run in a few hundred bytes. Whoever opens it races a translucent copy of you on the same wave, with the gap shown live. No server.
- **Clips:** the last ten seconds are kept in the background. Press C, or tap the button after a big moment, to save a 9:16 video with sound, or share it straight from a phone. Off by default on touch screens; switch in Settings.
- **Sky-rails:** shimmering aurora ribbons float high above the waves. Jump high enough to cross one and your board locks on and grinds to the end, with sparks, a little speed and some light back.
- **Shrine gates:** chains of three torii hang in the air. Thread all three in order for a Spirit Blessing: time slows for a moment, and for 8 seconds every trap becomes a bonus gem and rings are easier to catch.
- **Whirlpools:** surf into one and it pulls you under, spins you, and fires you out like a cannonball, faster than you went in. Fly over it to skip it.
- **Thunderheads:** in the Obsidian Storm, dark clouds crackle high overhead. Fly into one and its updraft throws you up through the clouds, where the sea goes silent, stars appear even by day and you hear chimes and a heartbeat.
- **The Leviathan (5 km, and every 5 km):** a sea serpent's scaled back rises as a giant ramp. Ride it and fly through its ring of runes for a big reward.
- **The eclipse (8 km):** the moon is covered, meteors fall and gravity eases, so jumps become long moon leaps.
- **A storm front** gathers over the last 2 km of the Mirage Straits, with sheet lightning, before the Obsidian Storm begins. Lagoons are foggy: visibility tightens and the lanterns light the way.
- **Razor moves leave an afterimage:** dead-centre rings, razor landings and near misses leave a golden afterimage and a chord. At x3.0 your scarf paints a glowing ribbon across the sky.
- **A rumble you can hear coming:** the tidal wave's sub-bass grows as it closes in.
- **Boards with their own trails:** Cinder leaves embers, Rime leaves frost mist, and the Starlight aura leaves sparkles.
- **The sea is deeper:** colossal serpent shapes glide far behind the waves, and ruined arches and fog banks pass at the edges of the frame, fading out toward the middle so they never block the view.
- **A title that is the world:** the game opens on a live, slowly panning sea with the name floating in the sky. Press to begin and it dissolves as the camera swoops down behind the surfer, already moving.
- **Layers:** far horizon landmarks, a slower dark sea behind the playable wave, the wave itself with rim light, foam caps on steep faces and shafts of light into the water, and dark reeds passing in front.
- **Things that live here:** sea birds perched on crests that burst upward as you carve past, jellyfish pulsing in the deep, schools of glowing fish that dart away from your board, lanterns bobbing on the swells, tattered banners on the sea stacks, and wind streaks across the sky.
- **Lagoons:** the last 2 km of every kilometre is calm: no traps, extra rings and gems, more lanterns.
- **The tidal wave:** at 3 km a wall of water rises behind you. Stay ahead of it for about 45 seconds, or get 3.2 km clear, and it falls away (+5 gems). Let it catch you and the light goes out. Never in Zen or during the first-run tips.
- **A rider with weight:** crouches low on a dive, leans back up a crest, throws their arms out at the top of a jump, and trails droplets and ripples when skimming just above the water.
- **Sky:** Settings can fix one of the five skies instead of letting them change every minute.
- **Dolphins:** keep a streak of three perfect dives and a pod leaps alongside you.
- **A living sea:** ripples on the surface, moon and sun reflections, glowing plankton below, fireflies above and the odd shooting star.
- **Four shores, four instruments:** Harp Shallows, Glass Reef, Reed Marsh and Deep Strings each play your melody differently.
- **Six auras** and **13 milestones** unlock through play.
- **A signature melody:** five notes that open your first ride, answer every fifth perfect dive in a streak and swell at the biggest moments.
- **Something hidden** above the rain.
- **Offline:** after the first visit it plays with no connection and installs to the home screen.
- **Comfort:** screen shake slider, flashes off, calm motion and lighter particles in Settings.
- **Kilometre markers**, a "past your best" moment, and tips on the first run that move on as you learn.
- **Seeds and postcards:** every wave has a link that replays it, and any moment can be saved as an image.

## How it works

- Vanilla TypeScript, Canvas 2D and the Web Audio API. No images, fonts or audio files: everything is drawn and synthesised at runtime.
- **Physics:** fixed 60 Hz step with two sub-steps and render interpolation, identical on 60, 120 and 240 Hz screens. Terrain height and slope are analytic sums of seeded sines.
- **Rendering:** each layer is drawn per art style and blended by weight. Particles live in a fixed struct-of-arrays pool, so nothing is allocated per frame.
- **Audio:** a compressor-limited master bus, a convolution reverb from a generated impulse, a speed-following pad and pentatonic notes quantised to an eighth-note grid.
- **Saves:** two localStorage slots with checksums and schema validation, plus a copyable save code.

## Develop

```bash
npm install
npm run dev        # http://localhost:5173
npm test           # physics, run rules, terrain, saves, music
npm run build      # type-check and build to dist/
```

`dist/` is a static site. It deploys as is to Vercel, Cloudflare Pages, itch.io (zip the folder) or a web-game portal.

## Hosting for income

GitHub Pages and Vercel Hobby are for non-commercial use. For a monetised build use Cloudflare Pages (free, commercial use allowed): build command `npm run build`, output `dist`. `public/_headers` already carries the security headers. Set `VITE_POSTHOG_KEY` as a build variable to switch stats on. Stats go through the game own `/ingest` path, which `worker/index.ts` forwards to PostHog (US cloud by default; change `POSTHOG_HOST` in `wrangler.jsonc` if the project is on the EU cloud).
